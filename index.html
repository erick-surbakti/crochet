About the studio

Slow objects for fast lives.

We work with natural fibres only: cotton, linen, undyed wool. Each piece is made start to finish by one maker and signed with a small knot in the final row.

We make in small numbers because the work cannot go faster. A collar takes an afternoon. A throw takes a week. We would rather you wait than we hurry.
